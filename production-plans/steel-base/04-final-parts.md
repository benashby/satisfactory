# Tier 4 — Final Parts

| Item | Rate | Goes to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Modular Engine** | **5/min** | **Final output** |
| Computer | 2/min | Adaptive Control Unit (all) |
| **Adaptive Control Unit** | **1/min** | **Final output** |
| Heavy Modular Frame | 1/min | Adaptive Control Unit (all) |

The Adaptive Control Unit line is the deepest chain in the plan and consumes the
entire output of Automated Wiring, Computer, and Heavy Modular Frame. Anything
that starves those three takes the ACU down with it — including backed-up Heavy
Oil Residue three tiers upstream (see [`01-smelting.md`](01-smelting.md)).
